# Chapter 16

Such a nice girl she is… innocent and zero ego.

Whenever we used to talk on the phone, I would always tell her,

“Mahechuuu, neeku manchi mogudu raavali… you deserve a happy life.”

She used to smile whenever I said that.

But the moment she told me that an alliance was coming on Sunday…

My heart skipped a beat.

Friday night. Weekend vibes had started everywhere.

For everyone else, it was just another weekend to have fun.

But not for me.

It was winter. Chennai doesn't get too cold—it’s just not warm, and there’s no humidity. That's it.

But I was sweating.

I couldn't sleep.

It was almost 4 AM by the time I finally fell asleep.

Saturday, she kept messaging me before and after her college timings.

I was just sitting in front of the TV, deliberately ignoring her messages.

Pratheep noticed.

“Enna machi… no chatting va?”

I simply smiled and left for the terrace.

That was our weekend spot. The place we went to calm ourselves down and relax.

None of us had dreams of going to pubs or anything like that.

We would just make lunch and dinner together, watch some movie on that small government-provided TV, and spend the weekend.

Mostly Sun TV or K TV.

Sometimes Sun Music.

And rarely Gemini.

Saturday passed by with me thinking about Mahechuuu.

Washing clothes — thinking about Mahechuuu.

Cooking — thinking about Mahechuuu.

Watching TV — thinking about her.

Walking out for tea — thinking about her.

Having pani puri — thinking about her.

And all this time, I was ignoring every single one of her calls and messages.

I had a habit of switching off my mobile while sleeping.

So when I turned it on the next morning, I saw 10 missed-call alerts from her and a few messages.

One message immediately caught my attention.

«“Babuuu Nityaa… match cancel aindhi. Adhi chebudhamani happy ga call chesthey lift cheyavey??”»

What else do you need to start a perfect Sunday???

I called her.

She picked up before the first ring could even finish.

“Pehh… em aipoyaav ninna antha Nitya?”

“Sorry Mahechuuuu,” I said.

“Pehh…”

“Velliponaaa aithe…” I said.

“Kaallu virghodatha.”

And then she continued,

“Kasepu avvaka chesthaane…”

I smiled and went back to sleep for another two hours.

Around 10 AM, I woke up to her call.

“Enti Nitya, padukunnava? Sare, malli chesthaane.”

“No… matlaadu,” I said.

“Inkenti mari visheshaalu…” she asked.

“Em matlaadavani… appude inkenti antunnav,” I said.

“Sareleeeeee…”

“Mmmm… cheppu.”

Then she asked,

“Emaindhi Nitya? Ninna antha em aipoyaav?”

“Chinna work undhi… ninna antha… so occupied,” I lied.

“Abbooo… aithe phone lift chesi cheppachu ga…”

“Mmm… cheppachu,” I said.

Then, trying to change the topic,

“Thinnava?”

“Thinna…” she said.

“Sare, nenu kasepu avvaka call chestha.”

There was a small pause.

Then she asked,

“Baane unnava Nitya?”

“Yeah yeah… absolutely,” I lied again.

She knew.

She always knew.

“Aithe pakka kaadhu…”

She guessed it right.
